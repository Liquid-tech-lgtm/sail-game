# 🌊 Welcome Aboard

**Salt & Sovereigns — Open Waters** is a top-down pirate trading game built as a single HTML file using HTML5, CSS, JavaScript, and Canvas.

You take command of a ship and build your fortune by sailing between ports, studying markets, trading cargo, accepting contracts, taking risks, and investing your profits into your ship and growing empire.

The game has grown from a simple trading experiment into a larger progression system with multiple ways to become powerful.

# 🎮 How To Play

## ⚓ Sailing

Use:

**W A S D**

or

**Arrow Keys**

Your ship uses momentum-based movement.

You don't instantly stop or change direction, so learn to:

* Accelerate
* Brake
* Turn
* Manage your speed
* Avoid reefs
* Plan your approach to ports
* Use the environment when escaping danger

The movement system is an important part of navigating the open water.

---

# 🏝️ Ports

Ports are where most of your business happens.

Different ports have different markets, specialties, factions, and services.

When you reach a port, you can access available trading and management options.

Some ports are associated with the Crown, while others are pirate havens.

### 🏴‍☠️ Pirate Havens

Pirate havens are particularly useful for activities outside normal trade.

They can also become locations for special events such as Black Market Auctions.

### 👑 Crown Ports

Crown ports provide access to legitimate trading and services, but the Navy presents an additional risk when you're wanted or carrying contraband.

---

# 💰 Trading

Trading is the foundation of the game.

Buy goods where they are inexpensive and transport them somewhere they are worth more.

The market isn't completely static.

Prices can move over time, meaning a route that was profitable earlier may not always remain the best option.

### 🗺️ Use the Market Chart

Press:

**M**

to open the full charts.

The Market section helps you compare prices across ports and identify promising opportunities.

You can also hover over ports on the minimap for additional market information.

### 💡 Trading Tip

Don't assume that the most expensive item is automatically the best trade.

Look at:

* Purchase price
* Selling price
* Cargo space
* Current market conditions
* Distance
* Risk
* Your available gold

A profitable voyage is about the whole trip, not just one number.

---

# 📦 Cargo

Your ship has limited cargo capacity.

Cargo can include:

* Normal trade goods
* Contraband
* Contract cargo
* Other special items depending on your progression

Managing your available space becomes increasingly important as your ship grows.

Some contracts also reserve cargo space, so accepting too many jobs can affect your trading plans.

---

# 🏴‍☠️ Smuggling

Not everything worth transporting is legal.

Certain goods are classified as **contraband**.

Smuggling can provide attractive rewards, but it also increases the danger of encountering the Navy.

The more wanted you become, the more dangerous your voyages can become.

If you're inspected, your options depend on the situation.

You may be able to:

* 💰 Pay a bribe
* 🗝️ Hide cargo
* 🏴‍☠️ Attempt to flee
* ⚔️ Fight during a naval encounter

Smuggling is a risk/reward system.

You can make more money.

You can also lose more.

---

# 🚨 Wanted Level

Your actions can increase your **Wanted Level**.

Wanted levels range from calm waters to increasingly dangerous Navy activity.

A higher wanted level can mean:

* More dangerous patrols
* Greater inspection risk
* Stronger enemies
* More difficult pursuits

However, the game gives you tools for dealing with the pressure.

Your ship, crew, equipment, legendary vessels, and navigation skills can all affect how you handle danger.

---

# 🚢 Navy

The Navy patrols the open water and can pursue wanted captains.

Navy encounters can happen while sailing or around ports.

Depending on the situation, you may need to:

* Escape
* Fight
* Deal with an inspection
* Protect your cargo
* Manage the consequences of being caught

The Navy also interacts with the reef system.

At higher wanted levels, navigating the environment can become an important part of escaping a pursuit.

---

# 🌊 Reefs

Reefs are hazards scattered throughout the waters.

Hitting them can damage your ship and disrupt your voyage.

But reefs aren't only obstacles.

They can also become part of your strategy.

A skilled captain can use tight spaces and reef formations to create distance from pursuing Navy ships.

The Navy is also capable of navigating around reefs rather than simply sailing straight through them.

---

# ⚔️ Naval Combat

Naval encounters are influenced by your ship and equipment.

Your combat strength can be affected by:

* ❤️ Hull condition
* 💥 Cannons
* 👥 Crew
* 🛠️ Ship upgrades
* 🚢 Legendary ships
* 🎯 Your current situation

Winning naval encounters can provide rewards, while losing can cost you cargo and resources.

Sometimes the smartest fight is the one you avoid.

---

# 📜 Contracts

Trading isn't the only legitimate way to earn money.

You can take different types of contracts, including jobs involving:

* 📦 Deliveries
* 👥 Passengers
* 📋 Supply orders
* ✉️ Courier work

Contracts can have deadlines and rewards.

They also give you another way to make progress without relying entirely on market trading.

---

# 👥 Crew

Crew members provide additional advantages to your ship.

Different crew roles can improve different aspects of your voyage.

Your crew system gives you another way to customize how your captain operates.

Choose improvements that match your preferred playstyle.

---

# 🛠️ Ship Upgrades

Your original ship can be improved over time.

Upgrades can affect things such as:

* ⚡ Speed
* 🚀 Acceleration
* 🔄 Turning
* ❤️ Hull strength
* 📦 Cargo capacity
* 💥 Combat capability

Ports also provide services that can help maintain and improve your ship.

---

# 💰 Passive Gold

Eventually, your money can start making money.

The game includes passive business ventures that generate gold over in-game days.

Passive earnings accumulate into a ledger that you can collect when visiting a port.

This creates another progression path beyond constantly sailing trade routes.

---

# 🏝️ Build Your Empire

Gold eventually becomes more than something sitting in your wallet.

You can invest in **Port Real Estate** and build businesses throughout the islands.

Properties can include production businesses and useful infrastructure.

Production businesses can generate existing commodities over time.

Other businesses provide useful bonuses at their associated ports.

Properties can be upgraded through multiple levels.

### Your empire can become another source of:

* 💰 Income
* 📦 Production
* 📈 Trade advantages
* 🏗️ Long-term progression

The goal is no longer just to have a lot of gold.

It's to build something with it.

---

# 📈 Trade Influence & Monopolies

Owning production businesses can give you **Trade Influence** in their associated commodities.

Influence can progress through tiers:

* No Influence
* Local Supplier
* Major Trader
* Dominant Supplier
* Monopoly

Higher influence can provide trading advantages such as:

* 📉 Buy discounts
* 📈 Sell bonuses
* 📊 More stable local prices
* 📜 Bonuses on matching Supply contracts

The system rewards players who invest heavily in particular parts of the economy.

You may even develop a reputation based on how you play.

For example:

* 👑 Sugar King
* ⚙️ Iron Magnate
* 🥃 Rum Baron
* 💎 Gem Tycoon
* 🚢 Shipping Baron
* 🏴‍☠️ Smuggling Tycoon
* 📜 Contract Merchant

Your reputation is derived from your existing progress rather than simply being selected from a menu.

---

# 🔨 Black Market Auctions

Rare auctions can appear at pirate havens.

These events are:

* ⏳ Time-limited
* 🏴‍☠️ Held at pirate locations
* 💰 Competitive
* 🎁 Full of rare rewards

Other captains can compete against you for the prize.

Rival captains have different personalities and bidding behavior.

You may encounter rivals such as:

* Reckless Pirates
* Wealthy Merchants
* Navy Defectors
* Treasure Hunters
* Rival Tycoons

Auctions contain three rarity levels:

### 🟢 Rare

### 🔵 Epic

### 🟡 Legendary

Possible prizes can include:

* 💰 Gold caches
* 📦 Valuable cargo
* 🛡️ Ship equipment
* 💥 Cannons
* ❤️ Additional hull capacity
* 📜 Legendary Vessel Blueprint

Auctions are intentionally rare.

If one appears, you need to decide whether it's worth changing your voyage to reach it before the deadline.

---

# ⛵ Legendary Ships

The game includes purpose-built **Legendary Ships**.

These aren't simply stronger versions of your normal ship.

Each one is designed around a different playstyle.

They trade strengths for weaknesses.

### 🌑 Shadow Sloop

Built around speed and escaping trouble.

**Strengths:**

* Extremely fast
* Strong acceleration
* Excellent turning

**Weaknesses:**

* Smaller hold
* Lower hull strength
* Weaker combat ability

---

### ⚙️ Ironclad Dredger

A heavily armored powerhouse.

**Strengths:**

* Huge hull
* Strong combat ability
* Additional damage protection

**Weaknesses:**

* Very slow
* Poor acceleration
* Difficult to turn

---

### 👑 Royal Galleon

A massive high-capacity vessel.

**Strengths:**

* Huge cargo capacity
* Strong hull
* Strong combat ability
* Reduced Crown inspection chance

**Weaknesses:**

* Expensive
* Slow
* Difficult to maneuver

The Royal Galleon also requires the **Legendary Vessel Blueprint**, which can be obtained through a Black Market Auction.

---

### 👻 Ghost Runner

Designed around avoiding danger.

**Strengths:**

* Fast
* Agile
* Excellent escape potential

Its special ability can make fleeing patrols less punishing.

**Weaknesses:**

* Weak combat ability
* Smaller cargo capacity
* Lower hull strength

Legendary ships are meant to give players meaningful choices rather than a simple "best ship."

You can own multiple legendary vessels and switch your active flagship.

## The current game includes four legendary ships.

# 🎖️ Empire Progression

Money isn't the only measurement of success anymore.

The game calculates an **Empire Score** using multiple aspects of your progress.

Your score can be influenced by:

* 💰 Net worth
* 🏝️ Properties
* 🏗️ Business levels
* 📜 Completed contracts
* 🏴‍☠️ Successful smuggling
* 🔨 Auction wins
* ⛵ Legendary ships
* 📈 Trade Influence

This produces a progression ladder:

| Rank                 |      Requirement |
| -------------------- | ---------------: |
| Unknown Captain      |                0 |
| Trader               |            4,000 |
| Established Merchant |           12,000 |
| Wealthy Captain      |           30,000 |
| Magnate              |           80,000 |
| Trade Baron          |          200,000 |
| Maritime Tycoon      |          450,000 |
| Legendary Captain    | Fortune achieved |

Higher ranks can provide small benefits and unlock additional cosmetic progression.

The important idea is that **there isn't one required way to build your empire.**

You can focus on whichever systems you enjoy.
