# 🏴‍☠️ Salt & Sovereigns — Open Waters

**Salt & Sovereigns — Open Waters** is a top-down pirate trading and exploration game built around sailing, trading, contracts, naval encounters, ship upgrades, businesses, auctions, legendary ships, empire progression, and building your fortune across the open waters.

This repository is primarily an **archive of older playable versions of the game**.

Each version represents a different stage of development. The builds are preserved so players can go back, play older versions, compare mechanics, and see how the game evolved over time.

# 🌊 What Is the Game?

You take control of a ship and travel between ports across a large open-water world.

Your goal is to turn a small operation into a powerful maritime fortune.

The basic loop is:

**Sail → Trade → Earn → Upgrade → Explore → Build Your Empire**

As the game develops, more ways to make money and progress are introduced.

You can trade commodities, take contracts, fight or evade the Navy, invest in businesses, participate in auctions, collect specialized ships, increase your maritime rank, and eventually pursue the game's Fortune goal.

---

# ⚓ Core Gameplay

## 🛳️ Sailing

Control your ship using:

* **W / ↑** — Accelerate
* **S / ↓** — Brake / Reverse
* **A / ←** — Turn Left
* **D / →** — Turn Right

The ship uses momentum-based movement, meaning acceleration, turning, and braking require some planning.

Reefs and other hazards also make navigation important.

---

## 💰 Trading

Visit different ports and trade commodities between locations.

Different ports specialize in different goods, creating opportunities to:

* Buy low
* Sail to another port
* Sell high
* Build trading routes
* Take advantage of market changes
* Increase your Trade Influence

Trading remains one of the main ways to build wealth.

---

## 📜 Contracts

Later versions introduce a dedicated Contracts system.

Contract types include:

* 📦 Cargo deliveries
* 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Passenger runs
* 📋 Supply orders
* ✉️ Courier dispatches

Contracts have destinations, deadlines, and fixed payouts.

They also contribute to your captain statistics and provide an alternative to simply buying and selling cargo.

---

# ⚔️ Navy & Naval Encounters

The open waters aren't completely safe.

Navy encounters can lead to dangerous situations where captains must decide whether to:

* Fight
* Attempt to escape
* Protect their cargo
* Manage wanted levels
* Use the environment to their advantage

Naval combat introduces danger tiers and potential rewards such as gold and salvage, while losing an encounter can result in cargo, gold, and hull losses.

---

# 🪨 Reefs & Navigation

Reefs are dangerous obstacles scattered throughout the world.

Running into them can damage your ship, making navigation an important part of travelling between ports.

Starting with **v2.0**, Navy cutters became reef-aware. They can steer around reefs, slow down in dense reef areas, and lose speed if they scrape rocks. This also creates opportunities for players to use reefs strategically when escaping pursuit.

---

# 🏆 Fortune & Progression

The game eventually introduced a much larger long-term objective.

The Fortune goal is based around reaching:

**1,000,000 Sovereigns in net worth**

The wealth ladder provides several milestones along the way, giving players smaller progression targets before reaching the final Fortune goal.

Your wealth can come from multiple systems rather than one activity.

---

# 🏦 Business Holdings

**v2.3** introduced Business Holdings.

Players can purchase stakes in business ventures and receive passive income over time.

Income accumulates while you continue sailing, trading, completing contracts, smuggling, and fighting.

The system was intentionally designed as a slower source of income so active gameplay remains important.

---

# 🏛️ Port Real Estate

**v2.5** expanded Business Holdings into a larger empire system.

Ports gained their own businesses, including:

* Rum Distillery
* Sugar Plantation
* Gem Concession
* Fishery
* Iron Mine
* Crown Shipyard
* Free Trading House
* Lagoon Warehouse

Businesses can be purchased, upgraded, and managed through the **Empire** tab.

Production businesses also generate stockpiles that can eventually be collected and brought aboard your ship.

---

# 📈 Trade Monopolies

**v2.6** introduced **Trade Influence**.

Players can develop influence over commodities produced by businesses they own.

Influence tiers include:

* No Influence
* Local Supplier
* Major Trader
* Dominant Supplier
* Monopoly

Higher influence can provide small trading advantages, including buy discounts, sell bonuses, steadier local prices, and bonuses on matching Supply contracts.

---

# 🔨 Black Market Auctions

**v2.7** introduced rare, time-limited Black Market Auctions.

Auctions can appear at pirate havens and require players to reach the correct port before the auction expires.

Players compete against rival captains with different personalities and bidding behavior.

Possible prizes include:

* Gold caches
* Cargo
* False Cargo Holds
* Cannons
* Hull improvements
* The **Legendary Vessel Blueprint**

There are three rarity levels:

**Rare → Epic → Legendary**

Legendary rewards are intentionally much harder to obtain.

---

# ⛵ Legendary Ships

**v2.8** introduced a proper fleet system.

Legendary ships are **purpose-built vessels**, rather than simple upgrades to your normal ship.

Each one has different strengths and weaknesses.

Examples include:

* 🌑 **Shadow Sloop** — fast and maneuverable but fragile
* ⚙️ **Ironclad Dredger** — heavily built but slow
* 👑 **Royal Galleon** — huge cargo capacity and powerful capabilities
* 👻 **Ghost Runner** — designed around avoiding trouble

Players can own multiple legendary ships and switch which vessel is their active flagship.

Only one ship sails at a time.

---

# 🎖️ Empire Progression

**v2.9** introduced an Empire Rank system layered on top of the existing Fortune goal.

Your rank is determined by an **Empire Score** that considers multiple aspects of your progress, including:

* Net worth
* Properties
* Property levels
* Completed contracts
* Smuggling success
* Auction victories
* Trade Influence
* Legendary ships owned

Ranks progress through titles such as:

**Unknown Captain → Trader → Established Merchant → Wealthy Captain → Magnate → Trade Baron → Maritime Tycoon → Legendary Captain**

This means there isn't only one way to progress. Different playstyles can contribute toward building your empire.

---

# 📊 Empire Dashboard

**v3.0** introduced the **Empire Dashboard**.

It provides a read-only overview of the systems you've built throughout your voyage.

The dashboard tracks categories including:

### 💰 Wealth

* Current Gold
* Net Worth
* Lifetime Earnings
* Fortune Progress

### 🏘️ Properties

* Properties Owned
* Property Value
* Passive Income
* Stored Production

### ⚖️ Trade

* Strongest Commodity
* Trade Influence
* Most Profitable Commodity
* Best Trade Achievement

### 💀 Bounties

* Bounties Completed
* Highest Bounty
* Total Bounty Earnings

### ⛵ Fleet

* Current Ship
* Ships Owned
* Legendary Ships
* Fleet Value

### 🔨 Auctions

* Rare Items Acquired
* Legendary Items Acquired

### 🎖️ Rank

* Current Empire Rank
* Next Milestone
* Progress Toward Next Rank

The goal is to give players one place where they can see everything they've built without changing the underlying gameplay systems.

# 📖 Captain's Manual

Later versions include a full in-game **Captain's Manual**.

The manual is organized into sections such as:

* Getting Started
* Trading
* Ships
* Navigation
* Combat & Navy
* Economy
* Controls
* Tips

This was designed to make the game easier to understand, especially for new players, without forcing them to read through one giant help screen.

---

# ❓ Ship's Dashboard Help

**v2.4** added a small **?** next to the Ship's Dashboard.

Hovering over it provides quick explanations for dashboard statistics such as:

* Gold
* Cargo
* Health
* Speed
* Wanted
* Lifetime Earned
* Net Worth
* Fortune
* Holdings
* Playtime

It is designed as a quick reference rather than another large menu.

---

# 👁️ Hide HUD

Later versions also include a **Hide HUD** feature.

Press:

**H**

to hide the main title bar and Ship's Dashboard for a cleaner view.

Important gameplay elements such as the minimap, charts, warnings, notifications, and docking prompts remain available.

---

# 🗺️ Maps & Navigation

The game includes navigation tools designed to help players understand the open-water world.

The **M key** opens the larger map/chart interface in later builds.

The interface provides access to navigation information and port-related information while keeping the actual sailing gameplay on the open-water map.

---

# 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Friends & Captain Profiles

**v1.8** introduced a local Friends system.

Captains can have a short friend code that can be shared with other players.

Friend codes can be used to:

* Send friend requests
* Accept or decline requests
* View captain profiles
* Compare statistics
* Compare leaderboard progress

Because the game is a local browser game without a backend, the Friends system is based on shared captain codes rather than a live online server.

# 🕰️ Why Keep the Old Versions?

This repository isn't just a folder of backups.

It is a **playable development archive**.

The older builds let players:

* Play earlier versions of the game
* Compare old and new mechanics
* See how the economy changed
* See how the Navy evolved
* Experience older progression systems
* Explore earlier interfaces
* See when new systems were introduced
* Follow the game's development over time

Some mechanics have changed significantly between versions, while others were expanded and carried forward.

That's part of what makes the archive useful.

# 🧭 How to Play an Archived Version

Each version is a standalone HTML build.

To play one:

1. Download or clone the repository.
2. Choose the version you want.
3. Open the `.html` file in a modern web browser.
4. Start sailing.

No complicated installation is required for the archived standalone builds.
