Salt & Sovereigns

A top-down pirate trading venture for a free period.

Steer: WASD or the Arrow keys. Forward to accelerate, back to brake, left/right to turn. Your ship carries momentum like a real ship — plan your turns.

Trade: Sail into any of the 8 port islands — each with its own look, faction and specialty. Ease off the throttle as you touch the harbour and the trading window opens. Buy commodities cheap in one port, sell them dear in another. Prices are frozen while you sail — they only shift when you Rest & Advance Time in a port, so the chart and tooltips always show exactly what you'll pay when you get there.

Survive: Reefs gash your hull — mind the dark rocks. Lose all your health and your ship is wrecked and towed to the nearest port, minus gold and cargo. Patch the hull at any port.

Charts: Press M any time for the full chart — every port, everything it trades, current prices, and whether each good is ▼ Low Cost, ◆ Fair or ▲ High Value right now.

Pause: Press Esc to freeze everything and open the pause menu — Resume, Quick Save, or back to this screen.

Smuggling: ⚠ Contraband (Royal Jewels, Black Powder, Cursed Relics) buys cheap in pirate havens and fences for 3–5× at Crown ports — but dock there carrying it and the Navy may board you: Bribe, Hide it in a false cargo hold, or Flee. Smuggling and fleeing raise your Wanted level (1–5 ★); each star is a Navy cutter that hunts you. They only chase while you're in their sight — open water and a head start will shake them (and drop a star), but at 5★ they match your speed. Pay off officials or buy false colours in any pirate haven to wipe the slate.

Goal: Reach a net worth of 10,000 sovereigns. The game auto-saves to this browser after every port visit.

Leaderboard: Your playtime and net worth are ranked against rival captains. Open Settings ⚙️ ➔ Leaderboard any time.
